Google Sheet setup

1. Create/open your Google Sheet. 2. Open google-apps-script.gs from this repository. 3. Replace PASTE_YOUR_GOOGLE_SHEET_ID_HERE with your Sheet ID. 4. Deploy the script as a Web App, execute as you, and allow access to anyone with the link. 5. Paste the Web App /exec URL into the builder. 6. Click Test Google Sheet. 7. Generate the form link.

The generated link contains the complete form configuration, so it can be opened from another PC/phone. Entries are saved to the connected Google Sheet and can be reviewed, corrected, deleted and exported from Entries.
