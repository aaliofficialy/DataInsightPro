Google Sheet connection

For a shared form to save entries from any PC, connect the form to a Google Apps Script Web App that writes to your Google Sheet. A plain Google Sheet URL alone cannot securely receive browser POST requests.

Use the included google-apps-script.gs: paste your Sheet ID, deploy it as a Web App, then paste the Web App URL above.
