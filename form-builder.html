How Google Sheet connection works

1. Create or open your Google Sheet. 2. Copy its URL or Sheet ID into the builder. 3. Open the included google-apps-script.gs, put your Sheet ID in SHEET_ID, deploy it as a Web App with access set to anyone with the link. 4. Paste the Web App URL into the builder. 5. Generate the form link and share it with your users.

The shared form works from different PCs/phones. Submitted rows are stored in the connected Google Sheet, and the Entries page lets you review, edit, delete and export them.
